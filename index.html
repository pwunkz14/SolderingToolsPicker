About this project

This site is a simple guide for newcomers and beginners who want to get into electronics repair — soldering irons, hot air stations, flux, solder, and console parts — without drowning in options.

Answer a few questions and you’ll get practical product suggestions matched to what you want to work on (phones, consoles, general electronics, and more).

Ongoing project

Expanding into a true all-in-one destination We’re taking the next step to make this the single place people go when they need tools. What’s changing:

Multiple stores — We’re adding support for many more retailers so users can compare availability, pricing, and shipping in one search instead of jumping between sites.

Bigger tools catalog — The tools list is being significantly expanded so more categories, brands, and specialized items are covered.

Reviews & ratings — Real user reviews and ratings will be added alongside every tool, helping people make confident decisions without leaving the platform.

The goal:

Everything in one place — discover the right tool, see where it’s sold, compare options, and read honest feedback without switching tabs. This moves the project from a simple directory into a complete decision-making hub for anyone looking for tools.
